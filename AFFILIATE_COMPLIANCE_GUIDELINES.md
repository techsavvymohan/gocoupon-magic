## 2. Strict Program Rules by Merchant

### 🏭 Alibaba WW (Admitad)
- **Zero Commission Geographies**: Alibaba **does NOT pay any commission** for orders from Chinese buyers, orders shipped to China, or buyers from **Mainland China, India, Russia, Nigeria, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria, and Ukraine**.
- **Core Incentive Countries**: US, CA, AU, GB/UK, FR, NL, DE, IT, ES, CH, PL, BE, SE, IE, AT, DK, CZ, MX, KR, JP.
  - **Extra Bonus**: +$14 bonus for New Buyer first order $\ge \$500$ in Core Countries.
- **Cookie Lifetime**: 60-day attribution window.
- **Excluded Categories**: Face masks, respirators, medical consumables, anti-coronavirus supplies, virtual products (gift cards, store credit), and US e-cigarettes.
- **Prohibited Traffic**: Pop-ups, pop-unders, slide-ins, interstitials, push notifications, brand bidding, incentive traffic.
- **Extension Notice**: Toolbar / Chrome Extension traffic must be submitted to the affiliate manager for category approval.

### 🏜️ Rayna Tours Many Geos
- **Commission**: 4.8% on top attractions (Burj Khalifa, Burj Khalifa with cafe, Dubai Frame, Green Planet, Dubai Parks & Resorts, Skydive Dubai) and up to 8% on other tours.
- **60-Day Active Rule**: If your personal promo code is not used at least once within **60 days**, it is automatically withdrawn/revoked by the advertiser.
- **Prohibited Traffic**: Brand bidding (strongly prohibited on all spellings/consonants), targeted ads (forbidden), adult traffic, doorway traffic.

### 👖 G-Star RAW Many Geos
- **Cookie Window**: 30 days.
- **5-Minute Basket Freeze**: Once a customer enters the checkout basket, the affiliate cookie is frozen for 5 minutes (no last-second coupon hijacking).
- **Voucher Geoblocking**: **Vouchers must NOT be displayed to visitors from Japan, Singapore, Taiwan, or Hong Kong** (vouchers are invalid in these regions).
- **Restrictions**: No pop-ups, no downloadable software, no trademark usage in domains or subdomains, only use affiliate-authorized coupon codes.

### 👶 Mamas & Papas (AE SA KW)

## 3. Universal Prohibitions Across All Programs
1. **Zero Brand Bidding**: Do not bid on any advertiser brand names (e.g., "Alibaba coupons", "Rayna Tours promo", "G-Star RAW discount") in Google Ads, Bing Ads, or any SEM channels.
2. **No Misleading Pop-ups / Click-unders**: Never force affiliate cookies via background tabs, pop-unders, or automated click triggers. Every affiliate transition must be an explicit user click.
3. **Trademark Protection**: Do not register domains or subdomains containing advertiser trademarks or common misspellings.
