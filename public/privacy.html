Privacy Policy & Chrome Web Store User Data Disclosure

Product: GoCoupon – Coupon Finder (Chrome Browser Extension)
Website: https://gocoupon.io
Last Updated & Effective Date: September 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the GoCoupon – Coupon Finder browser extension and the gocoupon.io web platform. We are committed to transparency, user privacy, and strict compliance with the Google Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies, the Chrome Web Store User Data FAQ, and the Limited Use Requirements.

1. User Data Collection & Access

GoCoupon adheres to strict data minimization. We only access the minimal technical data strictly necessary to test discount coupons and compare product prices on supported shopping websites.

1.1 What We Collect & Access:

  • Active Shopping Page Domain & URL: When you navigate to a supported merchant website (e.g. Amazon, Flipkart, Walmart, Target, Best Buy, Croma), the extension detects the current page domain to determine whether verified coupons or price comparisons are available for that merchant.
  • Coupon Test Results: When testing promo codes on a supported cart or checkout page, the extension notes whether candidate discount codes were accepted or rejected by the merchant form, in order to apply the highest savings code for you.
  • Local User Preferences: Your UI preferences (such as auto-apply toggles or notification preferences) are saved locally on your device via chrome.storage.local.

1.2 What We NEVER Collect (Zero-Access Guarantee):

  • NO Personally Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or national ID.
  • NO Financial or Payment Data: We never inspect, capture, store, or transmit credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, expiration dates, UPI PINs, bank accounts, or billing information. All payments remain 100% between you and the retailer.
  • NO Passwords or Credentials: We never access login credentials, passwords, session tokens, authentication cookies, or two-factor authentication (2FA/OTP) codes.
  • NO General Browsing History: We do not track, record, or monitor your web browsing on non-shopping websites, search engine histories, personal emails, or social media accounts.
  • NO Keystroke Logging: We never monitor general typing or form inputs. Interaction is strictly confined to entering discount promo codes in merchant checkout coupon boxes.

2. User Data Handling & Processing

Any technical information accessed by GoCoupon is handled strictly to provide its single, core user-facing functionality:

  • Single Purpose: Automatically finding, testing, and applying discount coupon codes and comparing product prices at checkout across supported e-commerce retailers to save shoppers money.
  • Local Processing: Coupon testing occurs locally in your active browser tab via sandboxed content scripts.
  • No Cross-Site Profiling: We do not build behavioral profiles, cross-device graphs, or device fingerprints of our users.

3. User Data Storage & Retention

  • Local Storage on Device: All user settings (such as UI preferences and cached coupon lists) are stored locally in your browser using Chrome's sandboxed chrome.storage.local API.
  • Session Data: Temporary coupon calculation data resides exclusively in browser memory during an active checkout tab session and is instantly erased when the tab or browser is closed.
  • Server Logs: Anonymized server access logs maintained solely for network security and DDoS mitigation are automatically pruned and deleted within thirty (30) days.
  • Immediate Deletion on Uninstall: If you remove the GoCoupon extension from Chrome (via chrome://extensions), all locally stored data, cached files, and preferences are immediately and permanently erased by the browser.

4. User Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

GoCoupon does NOT sell, rent, trade, or monetize personal user data under any circumstances.

  • Zero Data Selling: We do not sell user data to data brokers, ad networks, or third parties.
  • No Advertising Transfers: We never transfer user data to third parties for personalized, retargeted, or interest-based advertising.
  • No Credit Scoring: We never use or transfer user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending assessments.
  • Processors Only: We communicate solely with our secure, encrypted servers (via TLS 1.3 / HTTPS) to fetch verified coupon codes. Standard cloud hosting infrastructure providers act strictly as data processors under binding data confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Disclosures: We disclose information only if required by a valid legal subpoena, warrant, or court order issued by a competent legal authority.

6. Chrome Extension Permissions & Technical Justifications

In accordance with Chrome Web Store Minimal Permissions guidelines, GoCoupon requests only the permissions necessary to deliver savings:

Permission Type Exact Technical Purpose
activeTab User Action Allows the extension to read the URL of the active shopping tab when you view a supported store, enabling coupon testing on checkout.
storage Local Device Saves user preferences (auto-apply enabled, audio sound toggle) locally on your machine without transmitting them to external servers.
host_permissions Specific Stores Restricted exclusively to supported shopping domains (e.g. Amazon, Flipkart, Walmart, Target, Best Buy, Croma) to inject the coupon helper at checkout.

7. Affiliate & Commercial Disclosure

GoCoupon is 100% free for users. To cover infrastructure and engineering costs, we may receive an affiliate referral commission from merchant partner networks (e.g., Amazon Associates, Cuelinks, Skimlinks) when a user applies a valid discount coupon and completes a qualifying purchase. This never affects the price you pay and never involves sharing your personal browsing information with merchants.
