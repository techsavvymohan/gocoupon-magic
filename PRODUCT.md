# Product: GoCoupon

## Audience
- **Target Users**: Smart online shoppers across India and global consumers shopping on Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Croma, Ajio, Nykaa, and Tata CLiQ.
- **Problem Solved**: Eliminates fake/expired discount codes, detects artificial pre-sale price hikes, tracks genuine 90-day price lows, and compares live store prices in 1 click.
- **Core Value Proposition**: Real-time savings automation, zero manual code hunting, verified laboratory price tracking, and multi-store instant checkout intelligence.

## Experience & Brand
- **Product Lane**: Modern commerce utility & consumer intelligence platform (combining Buyhatke-style multi-store price history & comparison with Flash.co-style clean luxury commerce aesthetic).
- **Voice & Tone**: High-precision, transparent, decisive, frictionless, and respectful.
- **Anti-References**: Generic listicle coupon farms (CouponDunia, RetailMeNot), spammy popups, fake countdown timers, cliché purple/rainbow AI gradients, low-contrast text on colored backdrops.

## Core Workflows
1. **Multi-Store Product & URL Resolver**: Instant pasted product URL resolution (Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra) with live multi-merchant comparison.
2. **Interactive 90-Day Price Tracker**: Historical high, lowest ever, current price, and smart target price drop alerts.
3. **Automated Cashback & Coupon Engine**: One-click voucher testing and automated discount code application.
4. **Shopping Intelligence Spotlight**: Real-time deal scanner, flash discount alerts, and price drops.
